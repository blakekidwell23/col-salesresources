NorCal's Reversal Is the Story
NorCal is the only region with positive H1 growth (+2.3%), but that headline masks a recent turn: NorCal's 4-week average growth went from +1.2% (WK20–23) to -8.1% (WK24–27) — a real deceleration, driven mainly by Hayward (H1 leader at $2,993,748, +8.8% for the half) whose own weekly trend flipped from +9.8% early to -6.2% recently, and by Sacramento, which worsened from -3.8% to -12.5%. Santa Rosa has been negative in every one of the last 8 weeks and is down -15.6% for H1 — the most consistently weak location in the segment. Fresno is small-dollar and volatile (WK24 +181%, WK27 -55%) and shouldn't be read as a trend either way on weekly data alone.

SoCal and MWest are the recovery story. Both are down double digits for H1 (SoCal -17.2%, MWest -17.5%) but both have genuinely improved on a 4-week basis — SoCal from -5.8% to -3.0%, MWest from -11.1% to -2.1%. Within SoCal, San Diego is the strongest performer (+6% WK27, recent 4-wk avg +3.0%); Santa Fe Springs is still negative but improving (-15% WK27, recent avg -4.0%).

Net effect: the combined segment's 4-week average growth actually worsened from -2.5% to -5.9% — NorCal's reversal is outweighing the SoCal/MWest recovery. Worth a closer look at Hayward and Sacramento specifically before assuming the segment overall is stabilizing.
